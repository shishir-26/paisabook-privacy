1. Scope

This policy explains how PaisaBook Expense Manager & Tracker ("PaisaBook", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app on Android. PaisaBook is developed by Shishir Rakshit, an individual developer based in India. By installing and using the app, you agree to this policy.

PaisaBook is designed to keep your financial entries on your own device. We do not operate servers for the app, and we do not ask you to create an account.

2. Information and where it is kept

The information you enter is stored in a local database on your phone. This includes expenses (amount, category, date, and any merchant or note you type), budgets, subscription and AutoPay entries, split-group entries, money lent or borrowed in My Money Track (names, amounts, dates and notes), and app settings. We do not receive, access, or store this information.

My Money Track and your contacts

My Money Track lets you keep a ledger of money you lend to or borrow from people. If you allow contacts access, you can pick a person from your phone's contacts instead of typing their name. This is optional: if you deny the permission, you can still add people by typing a name. The names, amounts, dates and notes in your ledger stay on your device, and are included in your Google Drive backup if you turn that on. We do not receive your contacts or your ledger, and your contacts list is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

If you tap Message or WhatsApp to send a payment reminder, PaisaBook opens that app with a pre-filled message. You choose whether to send it, and it is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy. Similarly, if you send feedback from Rate Us, it opens your email app with a pre-filled message, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

3. Information we do not collect

PaisaBook does not read your SMS messages or notifications from other apps. It does not access your location, camera, or microphone. It accesses your contacts only if you choose to allow it for My Money Track. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account and the app saves a backup file of your PaisaBook data to your own Google Drive, in a storage area reserved for the app. We do not receive this file and cannot open it. Restoring a backup downloads the same file back to your phone.

The backup file includes your My Money Track ledger, so if you restore it on a new phone, your ledger comes back with it.

PaisaBook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

PDF and CSV exports are created on your phone and are saved or shared only where you choose.

5. Advertising

PaisaBook shows ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and app and device details to deliver, measure, and secure ads. PaisaBook requests non-personalized ads. We do not share your expense entries with the ad SDK or with any other party.

6. Third-party services

PaisaBook uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive (optional backup): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads): How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Play (app updates and in-app reviews): Google Privacy Policy

7. App permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, to run Google Drive backup, and to check for app updates.
  • Notifications: to show the daily reminder if you switch it on.
  • Contacts (optional): to let you pick a person for My Money Track. You can deny this and type names instead.
  • Run at startup, wake lock, and vibrate: to restore your reminder after the phone restarts and to deliver it.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad SDK as described in section 5.

8. Security

Your data is stored on your device and protected by your device's own security features, so we recommend using a screen lock. Backup files are stored in your Google Drive and protected by your Google account. PaisaBook does not encrypt the backup file, so we recommend a strong password and 2-Step Verification on your Google account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall PaisaBook. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing on our side to delete. A Google Drive backup stays in your Google Drive until you remove it.

10. Your choices

  • Turn off the daily reminder, or withdraw notification permission, at any time in Android settings.
  • Withdraw contacts permission at any time in Android settings. Ledger entries you already saved stay in the app until you delete them.
  • Revoke PaisaBook's access to your Google Drive in your Google Account settings, and delete the backup file from your Drive.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings, usually under Privacy or Google > Ads.

11. Children

PaisaBook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
